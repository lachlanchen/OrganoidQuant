## Interpreted panel logic
### Panel A
- longitudinal morphology/growth panel
- used to show the time-course effect of alginate on organoid development

### Panel B
- detailed condition comparison at day 7 and day 13
- consistent with transcript explanation that these days were pulled out for deeper analysis because one image per time point is too sparse
- likely designed to show fusion and differentiation differences more clearly at higher magnification or with more replicates

## Biological message of the figure
The figure is designed to support these linked claims:
- alginate slows early growth
- alginate delays differentiation-associated morphology changes
- control shows thicker / darker / more differentiated appearance earlier
- alginate groups preserve cystic morphology longer
- fusion exists in all groups, but its timing and appearance differ between control and alginate conditions

## What should be quantified for this figure
### Time-series metrics in panel A
- cyst size / organoid size over time
- area over time
- perimeter over time
- cystic morphology score over time
- differentiation score over time

### Detailed metrics in panel B
- day 7 and day 13 fusion score
- fraction of organoids with differentiated / thickened morphology
- confinement within droplet vs growth extending beyond droplet boundary
- representative frequency of connected or merged structures

## Figure design implication
The main figure logic is not only “growth under three concentrations”; it is a mechanobiology story:
- slower growth
- delayed differentiation
- altered fusion behavior

Therefore the final quantitative figure should include both:
- longitudinal growth lines
- dedicated late-time morphology/fusion comparison
